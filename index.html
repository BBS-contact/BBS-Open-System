Architecture & Process Mode

Current LEO architecture separates source evidence, evidence-derived interpretation, signal eligibility, analytical signals, process characterization, and human review.

SOURCE EVIDENCE -> EVIDENCE-DERIVED CHARACTERISTICS -> SIGNAL ELIGIBILITY -> DETERMINISTIC / STOCHASTIC SIGNAL COUNTS -> PROCESS MODE PROPOSAL -> HUMAN REVIEW
Signal rule: only signal-eligible SUPPORTED characteristics may contribute positive deterministic or stochastic signals. NOT_SUPPORTED, NOT_OBSERVED, UNKNOWN, and CONFLICTING remain distinct states and do not automatically create positive or opposite signals.

Canonical Process Mode States

DETERMINISTIC_PROCESS

Process context characterized by supported deterministic characteristics such as rule stability, procedural control, outcome predictability, or repeatability.

STOCHASTIC_PROCESS

Process context characterized by supported stochastic characteristics such as human discretion, behavioral variability, or response uncertainty.

MIXED_PROCESS

Process context where meaningful deterministic and stochastic characteristics are both present.

UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_REVIEW

Preserves uncertainty where available evidence does not support an adequate process-mode proposal.

Governance & Institutional Authority

LEO is designed as a human-controlled reviewer-support system. Technical capability does not automatically create institutional authority.

Human control

Human Review Required

Analytical outputs, Process Mode proposals, anomaly structures, reviewer-support information, and Reviewed / Institutional Knowledge remain subject to human review.

No enforcement authority

No Autonomous Enforcement

LEO does not independently convert analytical output into sanctions, restrictions, approvals, rejections, suspensions, punishments, or other consequential institutional actions.

No verdict authority

No Fraud or Legal Verdict Authority

Anomalies, inconsistencies, unusual patterns, or missing evidence may justify additional review but do not independently establish fraud, illegality, liability, or regulatory violation.

Production boundary

No Production Mutation

A completed demonstration, runtime baseline, test result, or public evaluation artifact does not independently authorize production deployment or production-changing actions.

ANALYSIS -> PROPOSAL / REVIEW SUPPORT -> HUMAN REVIEW -> AUTHORIZATION -> INSTITUTIONAL ACTION
  • Human review required
  • No autonomous enforcement
  • No autonomous institutional authority
  • No autonomous learning of institutional truth from raw evidence
  • No authoritative fraud verdicts
  • No authoritative legal verdicts
  • No automatic conversion of anomaly detection into accusation
  • No production mutation without explicit authorization
Knowledge rule: documents are evidence. Human-confirmed classifications and reviewed records may become institutional knowledge under controlled governance with preserved provenance, review context, and correction history.
